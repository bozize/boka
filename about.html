About BuildCoreServices

Practical infrastructure solutions for Somali communities and institutions.

BuildCoreServices is a construction and general services company focused on delivering dependable civil works, sanitation, solar infrastructure, rehabilitation, and technical procurement across public and community settings.

Focus: Community infrastructure Expertise: Civil works & public services Contact: +252 61 6822244

BuildCoreServices was founded to respond to the practical needs of communities and institutions requiring reliable construction and infrastructure services. Our work is guided by a simple principle: strong projects start with careful planning, quality execution, and respect for the people they serve.

From embankments and public sanitation to solar street lighting, rehabilitation, and equipment supply, we deliver solutions that help institutions and communities function with greater safety, resilience, and efficiency.

What we do

  • Civil works and infrastructure development
  • Sanitation and public-facing facility construction
  • Solar power and street lighting solutions
  • Public works rehabilitation and institutional improvement
  • Technical procurement and equipment supply

Our approach

We combine technical diligence with responsive project management to ensure every job is delivered with clarity, safety, and long-term value. Whether it is a public facility, utility improvement, or community infrastructure project, our team works closely with stakeholders to meet practical needs and build lasting outcomes.

Our experience across multiple project types reflects a commitment to trusted execution, sound engineering principles, and solutions that support everyday life and institutional service delivery.
