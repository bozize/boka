About BuildCore Services

Construction, engineering and infrastructure solutions rooted in local delivery.

BuildCore Services is a construction, engineering, consultancy, infrastructure and supply company based in Kismayo, Jubaland State, Somalia. The company works with NGOs, donor-funded programmes, public institutions, implementing partners and private-sector clients to deliver durable, fit-for-purpose infrastructure and technical support.

Legal name: BuildCore Services Based in: Kismayo, Jubaland State Registration: 20323

BuildCore Services brings together works delivery and professional consultancy. The company provides civil works, building construction and rehabilitation, sanitation infrastructure, solar lighting, procurement, equipment supply and project logistics as part of a practical service model designed to support durable community and institutional assets.

Its experience spans multiple projects in Jubaland, including sanitation works, laboratory equipment supply, embankment works, solar street lighting and rehabilitation of public infrastructure. The company also provides consultancy support in planning, flood-risk assessment, urban drainage and structural assessment.

Vision

To be a trusted Somali construction and engineering company known for dependable delivery, safe work practices and infrastructure that serves communities over the long term.

Mission

To deliver practical, technically sound and cost-conscious construction, engineering, consultancy, supply and logistics services through capable teams, responsible procurement, quality control and strong coordination with clients and communities.

Core values

  • Quality and technical integrity
  • Accountability and transparent communication
  • Safety, safeguarding and responsible delivery
  • Value for money and disciplined project control
  • Local participation and practical community impact

Our focus

BuildCore Services combines technical execution with project management, contract administration and procurement coordination. This approach allows the company to support clients across infrastructure delivery, technical studies, rehabilitation and supply-based services while maintaining clear documentation and responsible field operations.
