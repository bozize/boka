Flood protection

Flood Risk Feasibility Study

A corridor-level feasibility study examining riverbank, bridge, and embankment conditions to support resilient flood-protection planning.

Location: Kismayo–Barsanguni corridor Type: Feasibility study Focus: Flood protection and infrastructure resilience

BuildCore Services completed a flood risk feasibility study for the Kismayo–Barsanguni corridor, focusing on the relationship between flood behaviour, infrastructure vulnerability, and protection options.

The work included inspection of bridges, approaches, embankments, and riverbank conditions, as well as a review of hydraulic and geotechnical considerations. The study also considered potential flood scenarios, engineering options, and the preparation of costed recommendations for future design and investment.

Project focus

  • Flood-risk analysis and infrastructure vulnerability review
  • Hydraulic and geotechnical assessment inputs
  • Options appraisal and engineering recommendation
  • Prepared support for further design and implementation

By testing practical interventions against realistic risk conditions, BuildCore Services helped clarify the route toward more resilient flood management in a critical local corridor. The assignment reflects the company’s growing strength in technical consultancy and climate-resilient infrastructure planning.
