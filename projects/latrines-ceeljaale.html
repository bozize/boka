Sanitation infrastructure

105 Latrines

BuildCore successfully delivered 105 latrines across Ceeljaale One, Ceeljaale Two, and New Luglow, improving access to essential sanitation facilities in underserved communities.

Location: Ceeljaale One, Ceeljaale Two, New Luglow Works: 105 latrines Focus: sanitation infrastructure

BuildCoreServices has successfully undertaken the construction of 105 latrines across Ceeljaale One, Ceeljaale Two, and New Luglow. The project highlights the company’s ability to execute practical, high-impact infrastructure works that directly support public health, dignity, and community wellbeing.

Sanitation facilities are a critical part of everyday life and community resilience. By delivering this project, BuildCoreServices contributed to better access to safe and functional sanitation services in areas where these facilities are essential for health, hygiene, and social inclusion.

Why this work matters

  • Improves access to safe sanitation infrastructure
  • Supports public health and hygiene in local communities
  • Demonstrates BuildCore’s construction capability at scale
  • Provides practical infrastructure solutions that meet urgent needs

This project reflects BuildCoreServices’ commitment to delivering essential facilities with quality workmanship, coordinated execution, and a clear understanding of community needs. It is a strong example of how infrastructure can create measurable social value.
