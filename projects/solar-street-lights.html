Renewable infrastructure

280 Solar Street Lights

BuildCore successfully installed 280 solar-powered street lights across several communities, improving public lighting and strengthening local infrastructure with sustainable energy solutions.

Location: Goobweyn, Ceeljaale, Luglow, Buulo Bartire, Buulo Gaduud Works: 280 solar lights Solution: solar energy

BuildCoreServices successfully installed 280 solar-powered street lights in Goobweyn, Ceeljaale, Luglow, Buulo Bartire, and Buulo Gaduud. This project showcases the company’s capability in solar energy solutions and community-oriented infrastructure development.

Reliable lighting plays a major role in public safety, mobility, and economic activity. By delivering solar-powered street lighting, BuildCoreServices helped improve visibility, security, and everyday access across multiple communities while also supporting a sustainable approach to infrastructure.

Project impact

  • Enhanced community safety and visibility at night
  • Support for sustainable and energy-efficient infrastructure
  • Improved public access and usable urban space
  • Demonstrated capability in renewable energy implementation

From planning to installation, the project reflects BuildCoreServices’ practical approach to infrastructure delivery: well-coordinated execution, quality materials, and a strong focus on long-term value for communities. The solar street-light installation is a clear example of how the company combines engineering and service delivery for lasting impact.
