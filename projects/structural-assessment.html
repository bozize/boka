Engineering

Structural Assessment & Rehabilitation Design

A condition-focused engineering assignment covering structural inspections, risk classification, and rehabilitation planning for public buildings.

Location: Multiple districts Type: Structural engineering Focus: Public building assessment

BuildCore Services carried out structural assessment work for public buildings, with the aim of identifying risk, reviewing current conditions, and supporting evidence-based rehabilitation planning.

The assignment involved building inventory review, condition surveys, structural inspections, and practical technical recommendations for repair and improvement. This work supports safer building use, longer asset life, and better planning for rehabilitation and maintenance.

Project focus

  • Structural inspections of existing government facilities
  • Risk classification and maintenance prioritization
  • Rehabilitation design and cost-estimate support
  • Documentation for public-sector asset planning

Through careful field review and technical reporting, BuildCore Services helped public institutions better understand the condition of their structures and plan practical interventions. The project demonstrates the company’s ability to combine engineering assessment with programmatic action for critical public assets.
