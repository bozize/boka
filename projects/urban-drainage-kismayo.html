Urban Drainage & Flood Risk Assessment

A technical assessment and planning exercise designed to map drainage assets, analyse flood risk, and support more resilient urban infrastructure in Kismayo.

Location: Kismayo Type: Consultancy Focus: Drainage and flood-risk planning

BuildCore Services carried out an urban drainage and flood-risk assessment for Kismayo, supporting improved understanding of local drainage conditions, rainfall runoff, and vulnerable urban areas.

The assignment included drainage asset mapping, topographic review, hydrological analysis, flood-risk assessment, and engineering design support. This kind of work is critical for strengthening resilience, reducing infrastructure vulnerability, and guiding future investment in drainage and urban services.

Project focus

  • Drainage asset mapping and urban catchment review
  • Flood-risk assessment and vulnerability analysis
  • Technical design support and planning inputs
  • Documentation for informed infrastructure decision-making

By combining field assessment with technical analysis and design preparation, BuildCore Services contributed to a more structured understanding of flood risks and drainage needs in a key urban area. The work reflects the company’s broader capability in consultancy, engineering support, and practical infrastructure planning.
