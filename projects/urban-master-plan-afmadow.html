Planning & GIS

Urban Development Master Plan

A district-scale planning initiative supporting strategic infrastructure priorities, spatial analysis, and long-term development planning for Afmadow.

Location: Afmadow Type: Urban planning Focus: GIS and development priorities

BuildCore Services supported the preparation of an urban development master plan and infrastructure needs assessment for Afmadow District, bringing together spatial data, planning analysis, and infrastructure prioritization.

The assignment covered baseline data collection, GIS mapping, infrastructure gap analysis, flood and land-use review, and the development of a prioritized investment framework. This type of planning work helps local authorities and partners identify where roads, drainage, public facilities, and urban services are most needed.

Project focus

  • GIS-based district mapping and analysis
  • Infrastructure needs assessment for urban growth
  • Development corridor and priority zone review
  • Planning inputs for practical implementation

By linking spatial analysis with infrastructure planning, BuildCore Services contributed to a structured understanding of Afmadow’s development needs and future intervention priorities. The project reflects the company’s ability to support planning, land-use thinking, and practical public investment decisions.
